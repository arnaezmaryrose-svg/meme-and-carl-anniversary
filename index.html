How It All Began

It all started during the pandemic, when Meme was bored at home and decided to give Facebook Dating a try.

She had promised herself she wouldn't have a boyfriend until she graduated from college. But sometimes, life has other plans!

One day, Carl swiped right on Meme. At first, Meme was ready to swipe left because he wasn't even showing his face in his profile picture. HAHAHA!

But curiosity got the better of her, so she checked his profile. And then—SURPRISE! He was a FIREFIGHTER! 🚒

Suddenly, that left swipe became a right swipe. And just like that, their beautiful story began.

Five Years and Counting

What began as a simple match turned into years of love, laughter, memories, and growing together.

Through every season, may God continue to guide their hearts, strengthen their love, and bless their journey together.
